HOW IT WORKS

三步，建立你的连接。

Set up a connection you control.

查看 rclone 官方配置文档 ↗
Read the official rclone setup guide

  1. 在设备上配置 rclone

    安装 rclone，并使用你自己的 Google OAuth 客户端配置 Google Drive remote。

    Install rclone and configure a Google Drive remote using your own Google OAuth client.

  2. 在 Google 页面确认授权

    登录 Google 并检查请求的权限。文件内容和元数据用于你发起的传输和管理操作；实际可访问范围由你批准的 OAuth 权限决定，可能包含整个云端硬盘。

    Sign in on Google's page and review the requested permissions. File contents and metadata support operations you initiate. Access depends on the OAuth scopes you approve and may cover your entire Drive.

  3. 运行任务，随时管理授权

    令牌保存在本地 rclone 配置中。你可在 Google 账号中撤销 aibcaoo 的访问权限，并删除本地配置和文件副本。

    Tokens are stored in your local rclone configuration. Revoke aibcaoo's access in your Google Account and remove local configuration and copies when needed.

PRIVACY, EXPLAINED

知道数据去向，再决定授权。

此网站只提供应用说明，不接收 OAuth 令牌或云盘文件。文件传输由你运行的 rclone 与 Google Drive 及你选择的目标直接完成。开发者不运营用于接收这些文件或令牌的应用后端。

This website provides application information and does not receive OAuth tokens or Drive files. Your rclone installation transfers data directly with Google Drive and destinations you select. The developer operates no application backend to receive those files or tokens.
