### <a id="en-privacy"></a>Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-17

This Privacy Policy explains how this application (the "App") handles your information.

#### 1. Information We Collect

The App accesses your Google Drive through Google OAuth authorization. During authorization, Google provides the App with an access token used to access the Google Drive API on your behalf.

The App **does not** collect, store, or upload your personal identity information, file contents, or other sensitive data to any third-party server.

#### 2. How Information Is Used

The authorization token is used solely to access and manage your Google Drive files on your device via rclone. The token is stored in your local rclone configuration and is not sent to the App's developer.

#### 3. Information Sharing

The App does not sell, rent, or share any of your information with third parties.

#### 4. Data Security

You are responsible for safeguarding your local rclone configuration files and OAuth credentials. If you suspect your credentials have been compromised, revoke or reset your client secret in the Google API Console promptly.

https://policies.google.com/privacy
